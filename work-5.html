PROJECT STATEMENT
“A VISUAL ARCHIVE OF HYPER-PERCEIVED SENSATIONS.”

From the perspective of highly sensitive individuals, this series explores emotional overload and energy drain in social environments.

By conceptualizing emotions as “invisible monsters,” I translate visualized sensations into silhouettes and textures, where every fabric becomes a part of a monster’s skin—an embodiment of our inner holes and sensitive antennas.
