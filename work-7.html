Six looks.

03 / Celebrity concept

Almost an

A collaboration concept for Earl Cave: near-collisions and airborne mishaps hold each look on the edge of disaster, turning awkwardness into elegance.

04 / Music collaboration concept

Inner
rooms.

Joji and the band study become a private, awkward interior world. Each handmade hat translates a personality into atmosphere, then turns that atmosphere into image, object and performance.

05 / Craftsmanship

Make it in the
wrong way.

Craft is treated as an attitude rather than a demonstration of correctness. Repeated frames, misaligned bodies and visible joins keep the process present in the finished image.

From digital fragment
to stitched object.

Six caps close the loop between generated image and handwork. Raw seams, mesh, hardware and photographic fragments are kept visible as evidence of making.

06 / Project introduction

DraftPersona

Identity is treated as a public draft—continuously revised through imitation, resistance and play.

In dialogue with Martine Rose's language of subculture, masculinity and awkward beauty, the project moves between styled looks, spatial image-making, celebrity and music collaboration concepts, AI-generated motion, and hand-stitched caps. Distortion, visible seams and imperfect composites are kept as a working method: authenticity is never fixed, but rehearsed in public view.
