05 / Craftsmanship

Make it in the
wrong way.

Craft is treated as an attitude rather than a demonstration of correctness. Repeated frames, misaligned bodies and visible joins keep the process present in the finished image.

From digital fragment
to stitched object.

Six caps close the loop between generated image and handwork. Raw seams, mesh, hardware and photographic fragments are kept visible as evidence of making.

06 / Project introduction

DraftPersona

Identity is treated as a public draft—continuously revised through imitation, resistance and play.

In dialogue with Martine Rose's language of subculture, masculinity and awkward beauty, the project moves between six styled looks, spatial image-making, celebrity and music collaboration concepts, AI-generated motion, and hand-stitched caps. Rather than hiding errors, it uses distortion, visible seams and imperfect composites as a working method: authenticity is never fixed, but rehearsed in public view.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

An AI-powered collaborative imaging project in dialogue with the aesthetic of Martine Rose.

Starting from AI-generated imagery, this project hand-stitches a series of caps, merging digital textures with fabric craftsmanship and exploring the creative tension between artificial intelligence and the handmade. Each cap is a conversation held between algorithm and fingertip.
